- Repo: [walkinglabs/learn-harness-engineering](https://github.com/walkinglabs/learn-harness-engineering)

The harness is everything around the model: instructions, state, verification, scope, and session lifecycle. MIT licensed, in 15 languages.

## The course

14 lectures and 8 hands-on projects. Each lecture answers one question, like: why do agents declare victory too early?

## The files you can copy

Starter templates to drop into your repo:

- `AGENTS.md` (or `CLAUDE.md`)
- a feature list
- an init script
- a progress log

## The rules

1. One feature at a time.
2. Real proof before you say done.
3. A clean state at the end of every session.

It even breaks down how Claude Code and Codex build their own harnesses. The part I'd steal first is the one feature at a time rule.
