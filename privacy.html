Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy covers the Instagram messaging automation run by Prune Labs (@prunelabsco). When you comment a keyword on a Prune Labs post or reel, the automation sends you a direct message, checks whether you follow the account, and sends you the link you asked for.

What data is processed

  • Your comment's ID and text, and the post it was made on.
  • Your Instagram-scoped ID and username, as provided by Meta.
  • Messages you send to @prunelabsco in reply to the automation.
  • Whether you follow @prunelabsco (yes or no), checked only when you tap the reply button.

No other profile data, contacts, or followers are read.

Why

Only to send you the requested link and to avoid messaging you twice for the same comment.

Storage and retention

Data is processed on a private server operated by Prune Labs. Automation run logs are deleted automatically after 7 days. A minimal delivery record (comment ID, Instagram-scoped ID, username, status) is kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. Data received from Meta is used in line with the Meta Platform Terms.

Deleting your data

Send a direct message to @prunelabsco with "delete my data", or email the address below. Your records will be deleted within 7 days.
